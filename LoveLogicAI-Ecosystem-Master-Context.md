## 1. Executive Vision

The ecosystem is built around three strategic principles:

- IP-first: prioritize reusable, durable intellectual property over one-off output.
- Latency-Optimal Depth-Width Ratio: balance reasoning depth with breadth while minimizing latency.
- Sovereign Runtime: keep core execution under owned or controllable infrastructure whenever practical.

The broader intent is to build a sovereign agentic platform that can route tasks across local and remote inference surfaces, maintain shared state across surfaces, support GitHub-native workflows, synthesize human/market/agent signals, and package capabilities as reusable products and internal tooling.

## 9. Operating Assumptions

Assume the ecosystem is modular, sovereignty-oriented, latency-sensitive, and designed to accumulate durable IP. Poke stages intent, Soul-Forge executes or coordinates GitHub-native logic, SAK provides swarm decomposition, the infrastructure layer supplies model access and shared state, and Prism converts capabilities into reusable modules.

## 10. Compact Summary

LoveLogicAI is a sovereignty-first, IP-first agentic ecosystem centered on LoveLogic Soul-Forge, Poke, and SAK, with the Mac Mini M4 and Zo Computer serving as local and remote execution substrates. Cloud-Bypass, V2V Bridge, FlashHarness, and Shared State Spine (S4) provide routing, transport, harnessing, and memory continuity, while Prism, Crowd-Synth, Moonweft, Kalshi Integration, and V2V iOS Shortcuts represent reusable skills and capabilities. The lineage includes Archon, Secret Forge, the Sovereign OS-Level Proxy, Standup Bus, and Launch Manifests. Current active sprints focus on latency, throughput, local deployment, and internalization of code-scouting capability.
